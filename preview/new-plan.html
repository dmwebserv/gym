Pull-up ladder — level 3 of 7 · next target: 3 × 5 negatives at 5s

This is how it will look: collapsed at the bottom of every session, so it costs you no screen space. Tap it when you want to practise or log a set — the app times your hangs and your 5-second descents, and moves you up a level when you hit the target twice.

1Dead hang 3 × 20–30sGrip and shoulder resilience, and being comfortable in the bottom position you cannot yet pull out of.
2Scapular pull-ups 3 × 5–8The missing link. Hang, then pull your shoulder blades down without bending the elbows. That first inch is what most people never train.add this one now — it is the missing link
3Negatives 3 × 3 @ 5sWhere you are now: get up there and lower for a true 5 seconds. The app counts the seconds so you cannot cheat the descent by dropping.you are about here
4Negatives 3 × 5 @ 5sSame, more volume. Hit it twice in a row and the ladder moves you on by itself.
5Bottom-half reps 3 × 3Dead hang → pull to 90° → lower. Trains the exact range you told me you are missing.
6Band-assisted pull-ups 3 × 3–5No assist machine, so bands instead. Logged as a band level (heavy → medium → light), because bands cannot be measured in kilos.
7Full pull-up 1 repThen 2, then 3, then 5. You are closer than you think.

Optional accessories, any session: inverted rows 2 × 8–10 (the best single builder of your first pull-up) and jump-to-top holds 3 × 10s.

Weekly alternation

How the app will show which one is live today

Yes — the row always names the variant that is live today, in your colour, and the app decides it from what you actually did last time rather than from the calendar. No week A / week B to keep track of, and nothing to remember after a holiday or a skipped week. Both variants keep their own weight and their own PB.

This week — dumbbells (because you did barbell last time)

  1. 02✓ Flat Bench Press Chest 1/4 90–120s rest today: dumbbell ⇄ barbellDumbbell Bench Press 3 × 8–10 PB 30 · 12 Oct

Next week — barbell (the app switches on its own)

  1. 02✓ Flat Bench Press Chest 1/4 90–120s rest today: barbell ⇄ dumbbellBarbell Bench Press 3 × 8–10 PB 30 · 12 Oct

If you fancy the other one anyway, the log sheet has both chips and one tap switches it — and that tap is what decides next week. Same for the row (cable ⇄ machine), the incline press and the RDL.
